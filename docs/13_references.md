## Regulatory and exchange documents

- SEBI press release 22/2024 (23 September 2024): updated study of profits and losses of individual traders in equity
  F&O (93% lost money, FY22 to FY24).

- SEBI circular SEBI/DNPD/Cir-26/2004/07/16 (16 July 2004): market-wide position limits for single-stock derivatives.
- SEBI circular SEBI/DNPD/Cir-31/2006 (22 September 2006): MWPL linked to free float (20% of non-promoter shares).
- SEBI circular of 20 December 2007 (MRD/DoP/SE/Dep/Cir-14/2007): short-selling framework; naked short selling prohibited.
- SEBI Master Circular for Stock Exchanges and Clearing Corporations (16 October 2023), Annexure 3: securities eligible
  for short selling.
- SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MRD/MRD-PoD-3/P/CIR/2024/1 (5 January 2024): framework for short selling (all investors may
  short; naked short selling not permitted; institutional investors may not day trade; F&O securities eligible).
- SEBI (2013): extension of securities lending and borrowing to liquid non-F&O securities from 1 July 2013 (as reported
  by Business Standard, 30 May 2013).
- SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MRD/MRD-PoD-2/P/CIR/2024/116 (30 August 2024): eligibility criteria for single-stock derivatives.
- SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MRD/TPD-1/P/CIR/2024/132 (1 October 2024): measures for index derivatives.
- SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MRD/TPD-1/P/CIR/2025/79 (29 May 2025): measures for enhancing trading convenience and
  strengthening risk monitoring in equity derivatives (new MWPL, futures-equivalent OI, ban rule, entity limits).
- NSE Clearing circular 119/2025 (8 September 2025): standard operating procedure for the new MWPL, ban entry at 95%,
  exit at 80%, penalties.
- NSE Clearing, FAQ on position limits, limit monitoring and securities in ban period (updated 13 October 2025).
- NSE circulars FATAX56235 (STT from 1 April 2023) and FATAX73524 (STT from 1 April 2026).
- NSE surveillance circular 849/2025 (31 October 2025): intraday monitoring of the MWPL.
- SEBI press release 18/2020 (20 March 2020): regulatory measures in view of market volatility (MWPL halving, intraday
  monitoring of positions in banned stocks); BSE/ICCL notice 20200322-2 (22 March 2020) implementing it from 23 March 2020.
- SEBI press release 59/2020 (25 November 2020): review of the March 2020 measures (intraday monitoring of ban positions
  continued till further directions).
- NSE circular 1/2018 (July 2018) on STT for physically settled stock derivatives (0.1% on the settlement value, buyer and
  seller), as reported by Business Standard (19 July 2018).
- Indian Stamp Act amendments effective 1 July 2020: uniform stamp duty on securities (delivery purchase 0.015%, futures
  0.002%), as summarised by exchange-member bulletins of 1 July 2020.
- NSE transaction charges revised from 1 October 2024 (cash 0.00297%, equity futures 0.00173%), following SEBI's directive
  on uniform charges.
- SEBI (2024). Analysis of profit and loss of individual traders in the equity F&O segment.
