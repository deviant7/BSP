\section{The test plan and the data}\label{app:plan}

\subsection{The pre-analysis plan}
Before relating any outcome (the basis, trading, returns) to any treatment (ban status, utilisation, changes in the
limit, tax dates), I wrote a plan that fixes the samples, the definitions of outcomes and treatments, the estimating
equations, the predicted signs, the results that would contradict each prediction, and the method of inference. It was
frozen with a time-stamped tag in the project's version control on 5 November 2026, after my supervisor's go-ahead and
a full set of checks on the data. The plan has four primary hypotheses (H3, H6, H7 and H8), corrected jointly by Holm's
method at 5\%, and a set of secondary tests reported without adjustment. Standard errors are clustered by stock and by
date unless stated otherwise, and the basis is winsorised at 1\% and 99\% within each year.

Where the plan left a detail open (for example, the bins of utilisation in the trading test, the windows and placebo
dates of the cost test, or the population for the density test), I wrote the choice into a dated log and committed it
before running the test. Analyses added after a result was seen, such as the split of the trading test by enforcement
period and the noise correction of the threshold model, are identified as such in the text.

\subsection{Data construction}\label{app:data}
\paragraph{Bans and position limits.} The daily combined open-interest files give each stock's MWPL, its open interest,
its futures-equivalent open interest from October 2025, and a flag for stocks in which no fresh positions may be opened
the next day. Some files carry the year in two digits, a few carry another day's data under their own date, and some
archives bundle several days; dates are therefore taken from the file names and checked. The official ban lists are
missing at the exchange on three days; the lists for those days are imputed from the previous day's flags and continuing
spells, and marked.

\paragraph{F\&O membership.} A stock is in the F\&O segment on a day if its futures are listed that day. Symbol changes are
linked through each security's identifier (ISIN), so that a renamed stock is not counted as an exit and an entry.

\paragraph{Prices and the basis.} Prices, traded value and quantity, contracts and open interest come from the daily
futures and cash bhavcopies, which changed format in July 2024. The older futures files do not report lot sizes; I
recover them from open interest and check them against the day's price range. The futures volume-weighted price is
traded value divided by contracts times lot size, and the cash price is traded value divided by quantity. Dividends going
ex before expiry are subtracted at their present value. The interest rate is the monthly three-month interbank rate,
carried forward for at most two months when the latest value is not yet published.

\paragraph{Reproducibility.} Every data set is rebuilt from the raw files by scripts that report counts and checks, and
every estimate in this report is produced by one sequence of analysis scripts. A complete rerun from the processed data
reproduced every reported number.
